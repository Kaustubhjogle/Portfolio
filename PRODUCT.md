# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leads evaluating Kaustubh for full-time software engineering roles. Secondary: peers, potential collaborators, and anyone discovering him online. Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, job boards, search engines, or shared links — often on mobile.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio website for Kaustubh Jogle. It exists to convert a name into a conviction: the visitor should leave believing Kaustubh is an exceptional software engineer who ships real products with uncommon craft. Success is a recruiter reaching out, a hiring manager moving him forward, or a peer remembering his work.

## Positioning

Kaustubh combines deep craft obsession (UI polish, performance, code quality) with a proven track record of independently shipping products to real users — PromptPane on the Chrome Web Store, WhatTheCron live on Vercel. He doesn't just write code; he launches things.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically spend 30–90 seconds scanning before deciding to engage further. The site is a single-page application with section anchors (Intro → Experience → Skills → Tech → Projects → Education → FAQ). A downloadable PDF resume is available at `/Kaustubh_Jogle_Resume.pdf`. The site includes structured data (JSON-LD) and a noscript fallback for SEO and AI crawlers. Contact happens through GitHub, LinkedIn, or email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page portfolio, no backend, no authentication.
- Deployed to Vercel with Vercel Analytics. Domain: kaustubhjogle.site.
- Sections: Hero with typewriter role cycling, Experience (IIFL Finance, 2023–present), Skills (radar-style proficiency), Tech Stack (languages/frontend/backend), Projects (WhatTheCron, PromptPane), Education (B.Tech, Diploma, SSC), FAQ (structured data), Footer.
- No CMS, no blog, no API integrations beyond Vercel Analytics.
- Currently seeking full-time roles — "Available for full-time roles" is a live status, not permanent copy.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Kaustubh Jogle. Domain: kaustubhjogle.site. Both are permanent.
- Headshot asset: `/Kaustubh_Jogle.png`. Resume PDF: `/Kaustubh_Jogle_Resume.pdf`.
- The visual look is open to evolution or replacement; no aesthetic is locked.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real professional experience at IIFL Finance with quantified impact (5,000 Cr+ AUM, 300 Cr+ monthly disbursals, 25% speed improvement, 8+ module migrations).
- Two shipped projects: WhatTheCron (live at what-the-cron.vercel.app, source on GitHub) and PromptPane (published on Chrome Web Store).
- Education records with high marks (CGPA 9.58, 94.80%).
- LinkedIn recommendations available from past teammates.
- No fabricated testimonials, benchmarks, or client logos.

## Product Principles

1. **Craft is the proof.** The portfolio itself must demonstrate the quality of work it describes — performance, polish, and attention to detail are non-negotiable.
2. **Ship, don't tell.** Prioritise real evidence (live links, source code, quantified impact) over claims and adjectives.
3. **Respect the visitor's time.** Every section earns its place; scannable on mobile, deep on desktop, fast everywhere.
4. **Personality without noise.** Let character come through in the greeting, handwritten follow cue, and tone.
5. **Stay current.** The site must be trivially updatable as experience, projects, and job status change.
